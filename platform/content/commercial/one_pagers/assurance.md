# AEGIS Assurance — evidence you can produce on request

**From "we think it's governed" to an audit-ready evidence pack in hours, not weeks.**

## The problem

Evidence for AI controls is scattered across email, shared drives and tickets. When an auditor, customer or regulator
asks for proof, teams spend weeks reconstructing it — and some of it no longer exists.

## What it does

| Capability | Controls supported |
|---|---|
| Controlled evidence repository with retention rules and access control | SE-02 |
| Evidence labels on every item: Verified (named reviewer), User-asserted, Inferred, Missing evidence, Critical gap | SE-02, SE-05 |
| Audit-ready evidence packs per AI system | SE-05, GL-06 |
| Periodic control-testing programme with dated results and testers | SE-01 |
| AI incident management: detect, triage, contain, review | SE-04 |
| Post-deployment monitoring plans and drift thresholds | SE-09, SE-12, GL-08 |
| Governance KPIs for leadership, corrective actions to closure, management review | SE-03, SE-06, SE-07 |
| Hash-chained, tamper-evident audit log of material actions | SE-08 |
| Support for independent review and internal audit | SE-10 |

## Outputs

Evidence packs (Markdown/PDF/CSV/JSON) · control test records · incident log and post-incident reviews · KPI packs ·
corrective-action register.

## Who it is for

Internal audit and second-line teams; compliance leads facing customer due diligence or regulator inquiries; any
organization that has to show — not tell — how AI is governed.

## What you can verify in a demo

Generate an evidence pack for a system, see each item's label and reviewer, and verify the audit-log hash chain.

## Limitations

AEGIS organizes and labels evidence; *Verified* status always requires a named human reviewer. Evidence packs support
readiness and assurance; they do not certify conformity with any standard or compliance with any law.
