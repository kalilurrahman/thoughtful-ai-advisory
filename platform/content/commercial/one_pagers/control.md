# VERITRAIL Control — policies, gates and lifecycle workflow

**Turn your AI policy into risk-proportionate gates that people actually follow.**

## The problem

AI policies exist on paper; approvals happen in email; prompt and model changes ship without reassessment; exceptions
never expire. When someone asks "who approved this and on what conditions?", nobody can answer quickly.

## What it does

| Capability | Controls supported |
|---|---|
| 64-control library with owners, evidence expectations and indicative framework mappings | AO-01, AO-07, AO-08 |
| Prohibited and restricted use screening at intake | AO-10 |
| Tier-proportionate assessment templates and approval matrix | GL-02, GL-03 |
| Go-live condition verification | GL-07 |
| AI-specific change triggers (model, prompt, data, vendor, tool, agent) with reassessment | GL-04 |
| Exceptions with approver, expiry and compensating controls | GL-10 |
| Periodic review and recertification by tier | GL-12 |
| Retirement and decommissioning workflow | GL-09 |
| RACI, escalation paths and forum decision log | AO-02, AO-03, AO-06 |

## Outputs

Approval records with conditions · exception register · change-to-reassessment trail · forum decision log · policy
attestation records.

## Who it is for

Risk, compliance and legal teams running an AI governance forum; organizations moving from ad hoc approvals to a
repeatable lifecycle.

## What you can verify in a demo

Submit a use case, watch it triage to the right assessment depth, route to the approver for its tier, and record
conditions; then change a prompt version and see the reassessment trigger.

## Limitations

VERITRAIL workflows support your decisions; they do not make legal determinations. Integration with your ITSM or GRC
tooling is via API/CSV in the MVP.
