# VERITRAIL Register — the AI system of record

**One owned, classified inventory of every AI system, model, agent and vendor AI product.**

## The problem

AI lives in spreadsheets per team, vendor features nobody switched on deliberately, and agents engineers built last
quarter. Without a complete inventory, risk tiering, oversight and evidence cannot be applied to systems you do not
know about.

## What it does

| Capability | Controls supported |
|---|---|
| Single inventory of AI systems, models, prompts, agents, tools, data sources and vendor AI | EI-01, EI-05, EI-06, EI-07, EI-08 |
| Explainable risk tiering (factor-by-factor rationale, Inferred until a human confirms) | EI-02 |
| Named business and technical owners with acceptance | EI-03 |
| Purpose, users, affected parties and foreseeable misuse | EI-04 |
| Use-case intake and triage routing | EI-12, GL-01 |
| Shadow-AI reconciliation from procurement, API-key and SaaS discovery imports | EI-09 |
| Owner attestation and recertification on cadence | EI-11 |
| Third-party accountability and vendor AI records | AO-09, EI-08 |

## Outputs

Inventory export with counts and timestamps · risk-tier register with approval records · owner attestation records ·
agent tool/permission manifests · coverage metrics for leadership.

## Who it is for

AI governance leads and CIO offices with 10+ AI systems; regulated enterprises preparing for audit, customer or
regulator questions; platform teams that need an agent registry.

## What you can verify in a demo

Register a system through the intake fields, see its preliminary tier and the factors that produced it, confirm the
tier through an approval step, and export the inventory with evidence labels.

## Limitations

Discovery depends on the signals you can import (procurement, API keys, SaaS admin data); VERITRAIL does not scan your
network. Risk tiers are VERITRAIL readiness tiers, not legal classifications under any law.
