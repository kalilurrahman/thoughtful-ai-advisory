**Validate your AI governance position with evidence — and leave with a plan.**

The AEGIS Governance Baseline is a fixed-scope, 4–6 week engagement. It turns a self-reported picture of AI governance into an evidenced one: what AI you run, how risky it is, which controls are working, which are not, and what to do in the next 90 days.

## What is included

| Workstream | Scope |
|---|---|
| **AI inventory** | Inventory of up to 25 AI systems — models, LLM applications, agents and vendor AI — with owners, purpose, data sensitivity, autonomy and a human-confirmed risk tier |
| **Maturity assessment validation** | Your 24-question self-assessment reviewed against evidence; each answer relabelled Verified, User-asserted, Inferred, Missing evidence or Critical gap |
| **Five use-case deep-dives** | Full use-case risk and control assessment for five priority systems using the relevant [assessment modules](/framework/assessment-modules/) |
| **Two agent reviews** | Tool manifests, least privilege, approval points, injection exposure, containment and logging for two agents |
| **Gap register** | Every finding with AEGIS control citation, evidence status, severity, owner and SLA (30/60/90/180 days) |
| **Remediation roadmap** | Sequenced plan across the five pillars, with dependencies and owners |
| **Runtime policy drafts** | Draft AEGIS Shield policies for the reviewed agents, ready for Observe mode |
| **Executive report** | Board-ready summary of position, critical gaps, indicative framework readiness and recommended decisions |
| **90-day plan** | First 30, 60 and 90 days of actions, with named owners |

## How it runs

| Week | Activities |
|---|---|
| 1 | Kick-off with sponsor; scope confirmation; self-assessment completed by key stakeholders; evidence request issued |
| 2–3 | Inventory workshops and discovery; owner interviews; evidence review; risk tiering with your risk lead |
| 3–4 | Five use-case deep-dives; two agent reviews; runtime policy drafting |
| 4–5 | Gap register and severity review with control owners; roadmap and 90-day plan |
| 5–6 | Executive report; readout to sponsor and governance forum; handover |

Duration depends mainly on how quickly evidence and stakeholders are available.

## Who it is for

- Organizations starting or resetting an AI governance programme.
- Regulated enterprises that need an evidence-based view before an audit, a supervisory conversation or a board review.
- Companies deploying agents that want runtime controls designed before scaling.

## What we need from you

- An executive sponsor and a day-to-day lead.
- Time from owners of in-scope systems — typically short interviews.
- Access to existing policies, inventories, assessments, test results and logs.

## Important

The Baseline produces readiness findings and evidence, not a compliance opinion or certification. Recommendations that touch legal obligations include a recommendation for legal review.
