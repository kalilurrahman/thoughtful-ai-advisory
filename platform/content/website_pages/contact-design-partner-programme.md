VERITRAIL is early. We are working with a small number of organizations to test the product against real AI estates and real governance teams, and to shape what we build next. If you want influence over an AI governance platform — and are willing to tell us candidly what does not work — we would like to talk.

## Two pilot tracks

| | **Pilot A — Regulated enterprise** | **Pilot B — AI-forward SaaS or GCC** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Organizations in regulated sectors (for example financial services, healthcare and life sciences, public sector) | SaaS and product companies, global capability centers and IT services firms |
| AI estate | Roughly 20–100 AI systems, including vendor AI | Agents with tool access in or near production |
| Focus | Inventory, risk tiering, lifecycle gates, evidence and readiness against frameworks relevant to you | Agent and tool registry, runtime guardrails, human approval, containment and customer-facing evidence |
| Lead modules | [Register](/solutions/register/), [Assess](/solutions/assess/), [Control](/solutions/control/), [Assurance](/solutions/assurance/) | [Shield](/solutions/shield/), [Register](/solutions/register/), [Agent](/solutions/agent/) |
| Key controls | EI-01, EI-02, GL-02, GL-03, SE-05 | EI-07, IS-11, IS-12, IS-13, IS-15 |

## What partners get

- A Governance Baseline-style starting point: inventory, validated maturity assessment, gap register and 90-day plan.
- Early access to VERITRAIL modules and features before general release.
- Direct access to the team building VERITRAIL, including regular working sessions.
- Influence over the roadmap, control library and templates.
- Draft runtime policies for in-scope agents (Pilot B).

## What partners give

- An executive sponsor and a named day-to-day lead.
- Access to system owners for interviews and to existing governance documentation.
- Structured feedback at agreed checkpoints — including on what is confusing, missing or wrong.
- Optionally, agreement to co-author a case study once results are in. This is never a condition of participation.

## What we will not do

- Publish your name, logo or results without written approval.
- Describe your organization as compliant or certified as a result of the pilot.
- Take any high-impact action in your environment without your human approval.

## Express interest

Use the form on this page and mention "Pilot A" or "Pilot B". Tell us roughly how many AI systems and agents you run and what is driving your governance work. We will reply to arrange an initial conversation.
