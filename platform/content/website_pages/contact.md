## What happens next

1. We read every message; no automated sales sequences.
2. If a call makes sense, we propose times and an agenda.
3. We only use your details to respond and, if you opted in, to send the newsletter. See the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).

Please use a business email address. Do not send confidential information, personal data about others, or details of security vulnerabilities through this form; tell us you have something sensitive and we will agree a secure channel.
