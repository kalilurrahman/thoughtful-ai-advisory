Public-sector organizations use AI where the stakes for individuals are high and the duty of transparency is strong: eligibility for services, benefits and fraud detection, case prioritization, citizen-facing assistants. Public trust depends not only on getting decisions right but on being able to explain how they were made and how people can challenge them.

## Priority use cases

| Use case | Why it needs governance | Likely tier |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility and entitlement assessment for public services and benefits | Decisions affecting access to essential services; listed as a high-risk area in Annex III of the EU AI Act | High |
| Fraud and error detection | False positives affecting vulnerable people; fairness | High |
| Case prioritization and triage | Indirect decision impact; transparency | Medium to High |
| Citizen-facing assistants | Disclosure; accuracy; accessibility; personal data | Medium to High |
| Internal productivity and drafting | Data protection; records management | Low to Medium |
| Uses touching law enforcement, migration or justice | Specific high-risk and prohibited-practice considerations | Prohibited-review or High; legal review |

## Context, carefully stated

- **Transparency and inventories.** Several governments require or encourage public bodies to maintain and publish inventories of AI use cases or algorithmic tools; requirements differ by jurisdiction and change over time.
- **EU AI Act.** Public authorities can be providers or deployers. Certain public-sector uses are listed as high-risk, some practices are prohibited, and bodies governed by public law that deploy certain high-risk systems must carry out a fundamental rights impact assessment (Art. 27).
- **Administrative law and equality duties** may require reasons for decisions, non-discrimination and routes to challenge.
- **Procurement.** Much public-sector AI is procured, which makes vendor due diligence and contract terms central.

## AEGIS emphasis

- **Impact assessment (GL-02, AO-11).** Tier-proportionate risk and impact assessment with stakeholder input.
- **Contestability and oversight (IS-09, IS-08).** Human review, explanations and appeal routes for people affected by AI-influenced decisions.
- **Fairness and harm (IS-05, IS-06).** Testing for discriminatory outcomes and harm to vulnerable groups.
- **Inventory and transparency (EI-01, EI-04, SE-11).** Records that can support publication and public accountability.
- **Procurement controls (GL-11, EI-08).** AI-specific due diligence and contract terms.
