## 1. Who we are

The controller responsible for personal data collected through this website is [legal entity name], [registered address] ("AEGIS", "we", "us"). Contact for privacy questions: [privacy contact email]. [Data protection officer or representative details, where required.]

## 2. Summary

- We use **cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics**. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not store IP addresses for analytics.
- We collect personal data **only when you choose to submit it** — for example to receive a full assessment report, download the blueprint, book a Governance Baseline, request a demo, contact us or subscribe to the newsletter.
- We do not sell personal data.

**Please do not submit** personal data about other people, special-category data, secrets or confidential information in free-text fields or in the Ask AEGIS demo.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can also complain to a data protection authority. To exercise your rights, contact [privacy contact email]. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

## 9. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, redaction before external model calls and before log writes, and a tamper-evident audit log for material actions. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at [security contact] if you believe you have found a vulnerability.
