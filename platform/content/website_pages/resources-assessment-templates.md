AEGIS assessments are defined as versioned templates: every question names the domain it scores, the AEGIS controls it relates to and — for use-case questions — the evidence an assessor expects to see. That structure is what makes results reproducible and citable.

## Available templates

| Template | Version | Scope | Questions | How to use it now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI Governance Maturity Self-Assessment | 1.0.0 | Organization or business unit | 24 questions across 8 domains, plus 5 profile questions | [Take it online](/tools/maturity-assessment/) |
| AI Use-Case Risk & Control Assessment | 1.0.0 | One AI system or use case | Intake fields plus questions in 9 modules, selected by risk profile | Through the [AEGIS Agent](/solutions/agent/) or a [Governance Baseline](/contact/book-governance-baseline/) |

Editable spreadsheet and document versions of both templates are **coming soon**. If you need them now — for example to run a facilitated workshop — [contact us](/contact/) and we will share the current version.

## Maturity self-assessment at a glance

- **Domains and weights:** Strategy & Accountability (12%), AI Inventory & Visibility (14%), Risk Management & Lifecycle (14%), Data & Privacy (12%), Security & Resilience (14%), Fairness, Safety & Transparency (12%), Human Oversight & Operations (12%), Assurance, Evidence & Improvement (10%).
- **Scale:** 1 Not started → 5 Measured, automated and continuously improved.
- **Outputs:** level, domain scores, strengths, gaps, critical flags, 30/60/90-day plan, recommended controls, indicative readiness indicators.
- **Evidence status:** User-asserted.

Full method: [maturity model](/framework/maturity-model/).

## Use-case question bank at a glance

- **Modules:** core governance and lifecycle; data and privacy; security and resilience; generative AI and LLM controls; agentic AI and tool use; fairness, safety and transparency; human oversight and operations; vendor and third-party AI; monitoring, incidents and evidence.
- **Answers:** implemented, partially implemented, not implemented, unknown, not applicable.
- **Severity:** derived from control priority, confirmed risk tier and answer; mapped to 30/60/90/180-day SLAs.
- **Evidence:** each question lists expected evidence; answers remain User-asserted until evidence is reviewed by a named reviewer.

Full method: [assessment modules](/framework/assessment-modules/) and [gap analysis](/compliance/gap-analysis/).

## Using templates responsibly

Templates are a means to consistent evidence, not an end. Adapt wording to your organization, keep a record of the template version used for each assessment, and do not present self-assessment results as verified findings.
