Practical writing for people who run AI governance programmes. We aim for articles you could hand to a colleague as a working method — and we say where the evidence is thin.

## Launch articles

### [Why AI governance starts with an inventory](/resources/blog/why-ai-governance-starts-with-an-inventory/)

Every other governance control depends on knowing what AI you run. Why inventories fail, what a minimum viable record looks like, and how to get to coverage in weeks rather than quarters.

*Topics: inventory, risk tiering, shadow AI — EI-01, EI-02, EI-07, EI-09.*

### [Runtime guardrails for AI agents: a practical rollout model](/resources/blog/runtime-guardrails-for-ai-agents/)

Agents act, not just answer. How to design least-privilege tool access and human approval for high-impact actions, and how to move from Observe to Enforce without breaking production — with Contain ready for incidents.

*Topics: agents, runtime policy, human approval — IS-11, IS-12, IS-13, IS-15.*

### [Readiness is not compliance: how to read framework mappings](/resources/blog/readiness-is-not-compliance/)

A mapping from a control to a clause is a starting point for analysis, not a conclusion. How to use crosswalks across NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act without overclaiming.

*Topics: framework mappings, evidence labels, disclaimers — SE-05, AO-07.*
