A single document that sets out how to govern AI systems and agents with evidence — from accountability and inventory to runtime guardrails and assurance. Use it to brief your governance forum, structure a programme plan, or benchmark the approach you already have.

## What is inside

1. **The governance problem in five questions** — what enterprises are asked about AI and why they struggle to answer with evidence.
2. **Operating model** — roles, the governance forum, decision rights, escalation and resourcing (AO controls).
3. **The five pillars and the 64-control library** — objectives, owner roles, priorities and evidence for each control, with indicative mappings to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act.
4. **The maturity model** — five levels, eight weighted domains and the critical flags that override averages.
5. **Risk tiering and lifecycle gates** — intake, triage, assessment modules, approvals, change triggers, exceptions and recertification.
6. **Runtime protection for LLM apps and agents** — default-deny tool access, human approval for high-impact actions, redaction, limits, kill switch, and the Observe → Warn → Protect → Enforce → Contain rollout.
7. **Evidence and assurance** — evidence labels, tamper-evident logging, control testing and evidence packs.
8. **A phased rollout plan** — what to do in the first 30, 60 and 90 days, and how to sequence the following quarters.
