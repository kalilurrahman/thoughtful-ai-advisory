**This is a limited public demo.** It shows how the [VERITRAIL Agent](/solutions/agent/) answers governance questions: with citations to VERITRAIL control IDs, clear evidence labels and an explicit statement when it has no supporting source.

## Things to try

- "What controls should we have before deploying an agent that can send emails?"
- "How does VERITRAIL map human oversight to the EU AI Act?"
- "What evidence demonstrates an AI inventory?"
- "What is the difference between User-asserted and Verified?"
- "Triage a customer-service chatbot that uses personal data and a vendor model."

## What the demo will and will not do

| Will | Will not |
|---|---|
| Cite VERITRAIL control IDs for recommendations | Access your systems, documents or data |
| Label suggested tiers as Inferred | Make legal or compliance determinations |
| Say "No supporting evidence or framework source is available." when it has no source | Take actions, send messages or change anything |
| Show indicative framework mappings with the mapping disclaimer | Replace review by your own risk, legal and security teams |

## Limits of the demo

The demo is rate-limited and works only with public VERITRAIL content. Answers can be incomplete or wrong — review them before relying on anything, and treat every answer as a starting point for discussion with your own risk, legal and security teams. The full Agent works with your organization's inventory, assessments and evidence, inside your governance controls.
