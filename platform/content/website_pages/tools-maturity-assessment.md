**24 questions. 8 domains. About 8 minutes.** Find out how mature your AI governance is today, where your critical gaps are, and what to do in the next 30, 60 and 90 days.

## What you will be asked

First, five profile questions: industry, organization size, approximate number of AI systems, whether you use AI agents or autonomous workflows, and whether you place AI on the EU market or use AI affecting people in the EU. These tailor the critical flags and recommendations.

Then 24 questions — three in each domain:

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---|
| Strategy & Accountability | 12% |
| AI Inventory & Visibility | 14% |
| Risk Management & Lifecycle | 14% |
| Data & Privacy | 12% |
| Security & Resilience | 14% |
| Fairness, Safety & Transparency | 12% |
| Human Oversight & Operations | 12% |
| Assurance, Evidence & Improvement | 10% |

Answer each on a five-point scale, from **1 — Not started** to **5 — Measured, automated and continuously improved**. Answer for your organization as a whole, as it operates today, not as policy says it should.

## What you get

**Immediately, on screen:** your indicative overall score, maturity level (Initial, Developing, Defined, Managed or Optimized) and domain scores.

**In the full report (business email required):**

- Up to five strengths and five priority gaps, each citing AEGIS controls.
- Critical flags — for example, no reliable AI inventory, untested agents, or no kill switch — labelled *Critical gap*.
- A 30/60/90-day action plan.
- Recommended controls and AEGIS modules.
- Indicative readiness indicators for NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and selected EU AI Act themes.

## Read before you start

- **Results are self-reported and indicative.** They are labelled User-asserted because no evidence has been reviewed.
- **Readiness is not compliance.** The indicators help you prioritize; they do not determine conformity with ISO/IEC 42001, compliance with the EU AI Act or alignment with NIST AI RMF.
- **Your answers stay with you until you choose to share them.** The preliminary result needs no personal data. See the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).

The scoring method, levels and critical flags are published in full on the [maturity model](/framework/maturity-model/) page.

## After the assessment

The natural next step is to validate the result with evidence. A [Governance Baseline](/contact/book-governance-baseline/) turns User-asserted answers into a verified gap register and a remediation roadmap.
