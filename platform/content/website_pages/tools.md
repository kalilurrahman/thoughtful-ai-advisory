These tools apply the VERITRAIL Framework to your situation in minutes. They are free, they run against the same published control library and scoring rules as the VERITRAIL platform, and they are honest about their limits: every result is labelled with its evidence status, and none of them makes a compliance determination.

## Available now

| Tool | What it does | Time | Output | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [AI Governance Maturity Self-Assessment](/tools/maturity-assessment/) | Scores your AI governance across 8 weighted domains | About 8 minutes | Indicative score and level on screen; full report to download (business email required) | User-asserted |
| [AI use-case risk triage](/tools/risk-triage/) | Suggests a risk tier for one AI use case from a short intake | About 3 minutes | Suggested tier, factors, required assessment modules | Inferred — requires human confirmation |
| [Runtime guardrail generator](/tools/guardrail-generator/) | Drafts a runtime policy for an LLM app or agent | About 5 minutes | Risk profile, tool permission matrix, draft policy in YAML and JSON | Draft for review |
| [Ask VERITRAIL](/tools/ask-veritrail/) (limited demo) | Answers questions about the VERITRAIL framework and controls, with citations | — | Answers citing VERITRAIL control IDs | Framework content only |

## How we handle your inputs

- The self-assessment calculates your preliminary score without collecting personal data. If you request the full report, we ask for a business email, name, company and role, with your consent — see the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy/).
- Risk triage and guardrail generator inputs are used to produce your result. Do not enter personal data, secrets or confidential details; describe your system in general terms.
- Ask VERITRAIL redacts common sensitive-data patterns before any external model call, but it is a public demo: do not paste confidential information.
- We use cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics and do not store IP addresses for analytics.
